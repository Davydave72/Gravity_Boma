The home of Kenya's diverse culture

Urithi is a cultural centre and talent hub that preserves and promotes our diverse heritage while nurturing creativity among learners, artists and the wider community. We aim to complement the government's efforts in advancing culture, heritage and the creative economy.

From the Maasai communities of the Rift Valley to the Swahili culture of the Coast and the communities around Lake Victoria, Kenya's cultural diversity is one of its greatest treasures. This place exists to document, celebrate and share it.

Cultural regions of Kenya

Central Kenya

Kikuyu, Embu and Meru, with strong agricultural traditions.

Western Kenya

Luhya, Kisii and others, rich in music and ceremonies.

Nyanza

Luo traditions, fishing communities, music and storytelling.

Rift Valley

Maasai, Kalenjin, Samburu and others with pastoral and agricultural traditions.

Coastal Kenya

Swahili culture, coastal cuisine, architecture and maritime heritage.

Northern Kenya

Somali, Turkana, Rendille and Borana communities.

Our diversity is our strength, and our heritage is our identity.
